# RNA-Seq Accession Numbers

## NCBI BioProject

**BioProject:** PRJNA943415

The RNA-seq data used in this re-analysis were obtained from the NCBI Sequence Read Archive (SRA) associated with BioProject PRJNA943415.

## Selected Samples

For this guided replication, four RNA-seq samples were analyzed:

| Condition | Replicate | SRA Run |
|---|---|---|
| Control | Replicate 1 | SRR23869388 |
| Control | Replicate 2 | SRR23869382 |
| Salinity Stress, 24 h | Replicate 1 | SRR23869383 |
| Salinity Stress, 24 h | Replicate 2 | SRR23869381 |

## Experimental Conditions

### Control

Plants were not exposed to NaCl treatment.

### Salinity Stress

Plants were exposed to **200 mM NaCl**. The selected samples represent the **24-hour salinity-stress condition**.

## Original Study Design

The original study examined control and salinity-stressed faba bean plants at multiple time points, including 6, 12, 24, 48, and 72 hours, with three biological replicates per condition.

This re-analysis used a smaller subset consisting of two control samples and two 24-hour salinity-stress samples.
